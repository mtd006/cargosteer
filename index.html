Retail freight information

Compliance and customer operating guidance.

Use these process notes before booking cargo. They support clear handling and communication; they do not replace legal, customs, insurance, or regulated-goods advice.

Booking and commercial documents

Give accurate origin and destination details, cargo description, requested vehicle class, weight or volume information, declared value where requested, consignee contact, and pickup constraints. Review the quoted commercial terms before confirming the booking.

Customer recordKeep the booking number available for support and payment queries.
DocumentsProvide the invoice, e-way bill, delivery challan, or other documents required for the shipment.
Rate confirmationConfirm the charges and services included before a vehicle is dispatched.

Cargo, packaging, and restricted goods

Use packaging suitable for the goods, handling conditions, and expected transit. Do not tender prohibited, unlawful, unsafe, or undeclared hazardous goods. Special handling or regulated cargo may need additional documentation, approvals, packaging, and carrier acceptance.

Pack for movementSecure and label consignments to reduce avoidable damage and handling risk.
Declare accuratelyDescribe cargo truthfully; incorrect declarations can delay handling or affect liability.
Ask earlyRaise special handling, temperature, or hazardous-goods requirements before booking.

Pickup, weight, and vehicle handoff

Ensure the pickup contact, site access, loading readiness, cargo count, and supporting documents are available at handoff. Record shortages, visible damage, or exceptions at the time they are identified and share them through the agreed support channel.

Loading readinessHave the consignment packed, documented, and available within the agreed collection window.
Accurate weightsGive reliable weight and measurement data for planning and vehicle suitability.
Handoff proofRetain the booking, loading, and LR reference information for the shipment record.

Delivery, service exceptions, and claims

Delivery timing can depend on route conditions, loading and unloading readiness, regulatory checks, weather, and carrier operations. Review consignment status in the portal and report a service issue promptly with the relevant booking or LR number and available evidence.

Proof of deliveryConfirm recipient details and note any delivery exception where applicable.
ChargesReview detention, additional costs, or cancellation charges with the related record.
Issue reportingUse the support and complaints area to create an auditable service query.

Privacy, account use, and customer support

Account details, shipment contacts, documents, and payment records are used to operate the transport service and maintain service records. Keep client credentials private, use authorized contacts, and notify support if account information needs correction or secure review.

Keep access secureDo not share passwords, OTPs, or account credentials via unverified channels.
